Delete your Shopzyy account

Shopzyy by MannieDev

You can delete your Shopzyy account and the data linked to it at any time. This page explains how, and what is deleted.

Option 1: delete it in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Shopzyy and go to the Household tab.
  2. Tap App settings.
  3. Scroll to Your data and tap Delete my data….
  4. Confirm. You will be asked to sign in again with Google (or your password) so we know it is you.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Option 2: ask us to delete it

If you cannot open the app, for example because you no longer have the phone, email us at support@manniedev.com from the email address of your Shopzyy account, with the subject Delete my Shopzyy account. We will delete the account and its data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

DataWhat happens
Your account (email address, sign-in details) and your profile (name, avatar, colour)Deleted.
Households you created, with their lists, shopping history, trip totals, members and background photoDeleted for everyone in them.
Households you joinedYou are removed as a member and lose access. Items you added to the shared list may remain in that household.
Your push notification registrationDeleted.
Anonymous error logsKept. They contain no names, emails or list contents and cannot be linked to you.

How long it takes

Deleted data is removed from Shopzyy straight away. Copies can remain in routine backups of our cloud provider for a short time before they are permanently erased. We do not keep any other copy.
