How it works

From a sentence to a trip, in three steps.

1

Add it in a sentence

Type "2 kg rice, milk x2, eggs". Shopzyy works out the quantities, units and aisles.

2

Invite your household

Share a link or a code from any app. They join in one tap.

3

Shop together

Tick items off at the shop. Everyone sees it live, and gets one tidy summary when you're done.

Made for the people you share a home with

Everything a household needs, and nothing it doesn't.

Everyone sees it live

Add an item on one phone and it appears on every other phone in the household. No more double-buying milk.

Type it like a text

"2 kg rice, milk x2, eggs" becomes three neat items, with quantities, units and store aisles worked out for you.

Learns what you buy

Suggestions and restock reminders arrive before you run out, each with a plain reason.

Scan the receipt

Photograph a receipt to tick off your list and save the total. The photo is read on your phone and never stored.

One tidy alert

"Ada went shopping · 6 items." One summary when someone finishes, instead of a ping for every item.

Private by design

Sign in with Apple, Google or email. Your lists are shared only with the households you join, and you can delete your data any time.
