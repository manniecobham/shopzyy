Shopzyy by MannieDev

Need help with Shopzyy? Email us at support@manniedev.com and we will get back to you, usually within two working days. Please tell us which phone you use and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Common questions

How do I invite someone to my household?

Open the Household tab and tap Invite people. Share the link or the code. When they open the link (or enter the code in the app) and set up their profile, they join your household.

I used the original Shopzy app on my iPhone. Where is my data?

When you update, Shopzyy moves your households, lists and shopping history to your new account automatically. If something looks missing, open Household → App settings → Import from the old Shopzy app and run it again. It only adds what is missing.

Notifications are not arriving.

Check that notifications are allowed for Shopzyy in your phone's settings, and that Shopping trip alerts and Restock reminders are on in Household → App settings.

How do I change the language?

Shopzyy follows your phone's language. You can change it in your phone's settings: Settings → Apps → Shopzyy → Language (iPhone and Android 13 or later).

How do I delete my account?

Open Household → App settings → Delete my data…. You can also read our account deletion page.
