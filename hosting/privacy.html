Shopzyy privacy policy

Effective 14 November 2026

Shopzyy is a shared household shopping list made by MannieDev Technologies Ltd, a company registered in Nigeria (RC 8411822) and trading as MannieDev (manniedev.com), referred to here as "we" and "us". We are the controller of the personal data described in this policy.

Registration number: RC 8411822 · Contact: support@manniedev.com

In short

  • We collect what we need to run your account and keep your household's list in sync.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.
  • Receipt photos are read on your phone and deleted. They are never uploaded or stored.
  • You can delete your account and data at any time, in the app.

Who can see your data

People in a household can see that household's list, shopping history, trip totals and spending, and each member's name and avatar. Nobody else can.

Invite links and codes. Anyone who has an invite link or code can join that household until it expires, which is after 7 days. Share them only with people you trust. The invite page shows the household's name and the first name of the person who invited you to anyone who opens the link, including the messaging apps that show a preview of it.

We do not share your data with third parties for their own purposes.

Service providers and transfers outside your country

Shopzyy runs on Google Firebase and Google Cloud (authentication, database, cloud functions, push messaging and hosting). Google processes data for us under its data processing terms. Our database is hosted in the European Union. Some processing, such as running cloud functions and sending notifications, happens in Google data centres in other regions, including the United States. Push notifications are also delivered through Apple's and Google's notification services. If you sign in with Google or Apple, those companies handle that sign-in under their own privacy policies.

When data is transferred outside the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Nigeria, it is protected by the safeguards in Google's data processing terms, which include the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Account, profile and content: until you delete your account. After that it is removed straight away from our live systems, and copies in our cloud provider's routine backups are erased shortly afterwards.
  • Households you only joined: items you added may stay in that household. See the account deletion page.
  • Anonymous error logs: may be kept after you delete your account, because they cannot be linked to you.
  • Website logs: kept by our hosting provider under its own retention schedule.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including the European Union, the United Kingdom and Nigeria, you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of your data (access and portability);
  • correct data that is wrong (rectification);
  • delete your data (erasure);
  • limit how we use it (restriction);
  • stop using it where we rely on legitimate interests (objection).

You can withdraw consent at any time, for example by turning off notifications or by removing camera or photo access in your phone's settings. We do not make decisions about you using fully automated processing that has legal or similar effects.

Several of these you can do yourself in the app: edit your profile, turn off notifications, and delete your account and data (Household → App settings → Delete my data). For anything else, email support@manniedev.com. We will reply within one month. We may need to check your identity first.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority, for example the Nigeria Data Protection Commission, your national authority in the European Union, or the Information Commissioner's Office in the United Kingdom.

US state privacy rights

We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we use it only to provide Shopzyy. If you live in California or another US state with a privacy law, you can ask us to tell you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. We will not treat you differently for doing so. Use the contact details above.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest by our cloud provider, and protected by access rules so that only the members of a household can read its content. If a data breach is likely to put you at risk, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Children

Shopzyy is not directed at children and is intended for people aged 18 and over. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app before they take effect. Earlier versions are available on request.
