Shopzyy privacy policy

Effective 12 November 2026

Shopzyy is a shared household shopping list made by MannieDev ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps.

What we collect

DataWhy
Account: email address and sign-in details (through Google, Apple or email and password); a user IDTo create your account and sign you in.
Profile: the name, avatar and colour you chooseSo the people in your households can see who added or bought what.
Your content: households, shopping lists, items, shopping history, trip totals you enter, and an optional household background photoTo provide the app and keep it in sync on your members' phones.
Device: a push notification token, your phone's language, app version and platformTo send the alerts you turn on, in your language, and to keep the app working.
Diagnostics: anonymous technical error logsTo find and fix problems. They contain no names, emails, household names or list contents.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos other than a background photo you choose, or any payment information.

Who can see your data

People you invite to a household can see that household's list, history and members' names and avatars. Nobody else can. We do not share your data with third parties for their own purposes.

Service providers

Shopzyy runs on Google Firebase and Google Cloud (authentication, database, cloud functions and push messaging), which process data for us under Google's terms. Our database is hosted in the European Union; some processing happens in Google data centres in other regions, including the United States. If you sign in with Google or Apple, those companies handle that sign-in under their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

We keep your data until you delete your account. Anonymous error logs may be kept after that because they cannot be linked to you.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account and data in the app (Household → App settings → Delete my data) or by following the steps on our account deletion page.
  • Edit your profile in the app at any time.
  • Notifications can be turned off in the app's settings or your phone's settings.
  • Depending on where you live (for example in Nigeria, the EU or the UK), you can also ask us to access, correct, export or restrict the use of your data. Email us and we will reply within 30 days.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and protected by access rules so that only the members of a household can read it.

Children

Shopzyy is not directed at children and is intended for people aged 18 and over. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.

Terms

Using Shopzyy is also subject to our terms of use.
